# Pedagogue consultation — כותבים מילים (א׳ תות)

**Expert:** Early-literacy pedagogue / grade-1 reading-writing teacher  
**Date:** 2026-10-30 (Asia/Jerusalem)  
**Brief:** First words children learn to read & write; general pictures (dog, cat, flower, colors…); same mechanic as family-names.

## Decisions

1. **Niqqud:** Do **not** show niqqud on tiles or on the completed word. Grade-א׳ letter-building practice matches writing (consonants + matres). Pronunciation comes from audio. Showing niqqud next to bare tiles would confuse.
2. **Spelling:** Use **standard school spelling** taught in grade 1 (ktiv haser / accepted forms). Adi’s standing plene preference yields when it conflicts (e.g. בית not ביית, כלב not כילב). Keep ו/י as taught (חתול with ו, כחול with ו).
3. **Final letters (sofit):** Use correct sofit when word-final. Selected list avoids awkward sofit-only words for early rounds; none of the 24 need sofit.
4. **No digraphs** (no צ׳) — class tool should stay within the 22 letters.
5. **Picturable & unambiguous:** concrete nouns + four basic color words shown as big color swatches.
6. **Run length:** 6 random words/game, soft-start shorter first (same as family).
7. **Levels:** Keep family L1–L4 ladder (one missing → one shown → all free → ordered).
